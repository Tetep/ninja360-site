## 2. White Belt — scope

stills, one drone exterior if airspace permits (48-h FAA lead) · tour published to GBP Street View
+ hosted on Kuula white-label · GBP optimization pass (categories, hours incl. holiday, NAP verified,
links, services/attributes, ≤750-char description, photos ordered, logo + cover, 3 seeded FAQs) ·
"Inside [Business]" page on ninja360.net · go-live email with every link.

**Monthly ("Visibility Upkeep", by the 15th):** one GBP post · reply to every new review in the
owner's voice, flag ≤3★ to owner by text same day · listing health check (revert Google/customer
edits, tour loads) · one-line report with GBP Insights views.

**Not included:** new filming, video editing, social media, website work, citations beyond Google,
seasonal refresh, FPV, GHL automations, review solicitation.

**Client provides:** GBP manager access · logo · hours/menu · a contact who approves posts by text
within 48 h.
